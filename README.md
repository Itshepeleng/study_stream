# StudyStream

StudyStream is a study collaboration platform designed to alleviate the lack of focus, isolation, and poor organisation often experienced in remote and hybrid learning environments. By integrating community-based features with modern productivity tools, StudyStream creates a supportive digital space that enhances the learning experience for students.

---

## Project Overview

In a world dominated by hybrid learning, many students struggle with short attention spans driven by social media, making it difficult to concentrate for extended periods. **StudyStream** provides a centralized space where high school, college, and university learners can access interactive study tools, track their academic progress, and connect with peers to build accountability.

### Key Goals
* **Organise:** Streamline and structure study activities.
* **Focus:** Encourage deep work through focus timers and distraction blockers.
* **Support:** Deliver personalised, AI-driven study support.
* **Motivate:** Keep users engaged using robust accountability systems and gamification.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Live Study Rooms
Virtual co-working spaces engineered for community-focused productivity.
* **Split-Screen Interface:** Keep your camera/video feed active next to a shared workspace.
* **Group Timers:** Coordinate study intervals with peers using built-in timers.
* **Deep Focus Mode:** Instantly hide chats and video feeds to eliminate distractions.
* **Communication:** Embedded low-latency audio and video streams via WebRTC.

### 2. AI Study Assistant
Your personalised digital tutor powered by modern AI processing APIs.
* **Smart Summaries:** Automatically condenses long textbooks and study notes.
* **Concept Explanations:** Breaks down complex academic notes into easily understandable material.
* **Interactive Quizzes:** Generates custom practice questions and flashcards directly from your text inputs.

### 3. Collaborative Notes
Real-time shared documents to seamlessly exchange knowledge with fellow learners.
* **Real-time Syncing:** Co-author notes with other users simultaneously.
* **Cloud Integration:** Auto-syncs directly to third-party storage platforms like Google Drive and OneDrive.

### 4. Accountability & Gamified Progress
Stay on track and driven through clear visual feedback and community milestones.
* **Visual Progress:** Deep dive into your learning habits with interactive graphs tracking your total study hours.
* **Reward Systems:** Earn motivational badges, build study streaks, and participate in global challenges.
* **Educational Gaming:** Play interactive learning games specifically designed to help retain your study content.

---

## UI & Tech Stack Requirements

* **User Interface:** Minimalist and modern layout incorporating a signature **Teal, White, and Warm Yellow** colour palette. Built with accessibility in mind (including Screen Reader support and high-contrast modes).
* **Communications:** Secure HTTPS protocol for data transfer and WebRTC for live audio/video communication.
* **Third-Party APIs:** Integration with AI engines (e.g., OpenAI or Azure AI) and optional Chrome Extensions for deep system-level distraction blocking.
* **Performance Targets:** Live study rooms optimized for ultra-low latency (< 200ms) with automated session reconnection during network drops.
